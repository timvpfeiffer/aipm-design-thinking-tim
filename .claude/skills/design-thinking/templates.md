# Artefact Templates

Every artefact starts with:

**Evidence status:** (for example "No real research yet. All cells are assumptions.")

## Evidence log

| Source | Type | Wording or data | Context | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Type: direct / behavioural / interpretation / assumption.

## Persona

- User group and context
- Goal and the situation that creates it
- Relevant behaviours and constraints
- Evidence-backed needs or pain points
- Open questions where evidence is weak

Avoid unsupported demographics, stereotypes, invented quotes and false precision.

## Journey map

Actor, scenario, goal. Then 4 to 6 phases:

| Phase | Action | Thought | Emotion | Evidence or assumption |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Then one pain point and one opportunity. Do not design a feature.

## Problem frame

- Clusters and tensions (with evidence labels)
- POV: [User] needs [need] because [insight from evidence].
- Problem statement
- HMW questions: How might we help [user] [progress] when [context]?
- Constraints

## User story

As a [user], I want [action], so that [benefit].
Evidence attached. Learning question.

## Prototype brief

- Learning question
- Assumptions under test
- Fidelity and why
- Flow

## Test plan

- Learning question
- Participants and why they are relevant
- Neutral task: "Imagine you are [person in context]. Show me how you would
  [goal] using this prototype."
- What to observe
- Follow-up questions
- What evidence would change the design
