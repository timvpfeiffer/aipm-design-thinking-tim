# Journey Map: Booking a GP Appointment

**Actor:** Working adult (25–40) with a non-urgent health issue
**Goal:** Get an appointment that fits around work, without a long wait
**Scenario:** Two weeks of nagging back pain, and they finally decide to book

**Evidence status:** No real research yet. Every evidence cell is an **assumption (A)**. This is a hypothesis map, not a finding.

## Journey

| Phase                       | Action                                                          | Thought                                  | Emotion                   | Evidence / assumption                                                          |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1. Realise**        | Notices the pain is not going away and searches symptoms online | "Is this serious, or am I overreacting?" | Worried, unsure           | **A:** People delay booking because they doubt it is "serious enough"    |
| **2. Find**           | Looks up the practice's phone number or website                 | "Where do I even book this?"             | Mildly annoyed            | **A:** Booking info is hard to find on practice websites                 |
| **3. Choose**         | Calls in the morning, or tries an online form                   | "I can't wait on hold during work."      | Frustrated                | **A:** Phone lines are busy in the morning, and online slots are limited |
| **4. Confirm**        | Gets a slot, then looks for a confirmation                      | "Did it actually go through?"            | Relieved, with some doubt | **A:** Confirmations are sometimes unclear or arrive late                |
| **5. Prepare and go** | Gets a reminder, maybe needs to reschedule                      | "What if I can't make it?"               | Anxious                   | **A:** Rescheduling is harder than booking                               |

## Pain point

**Phase 3 (Choose):** Getting through to book at a time that fits work.

## Opportunity

Let people book outside office hours, in a few taps, without waiting on hold.

This describes an opportunity, not a solution. No feature has been chosen.

## Open questions
